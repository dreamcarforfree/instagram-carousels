METHOD 05 • PART B

Retention Arbitrage

The Challenge
Driving organic web traffic is expensive; keeping users engaged on-site is where the actual profitability is captured.
The Multiplier
Simple, browser-based puzzle or guessing games hold user attention for 20+ minutes, multiplying native ad impressions with zero extra acquisition cost.
Engagement multiplies impressions. Impressions multiply recurring ad returns.
