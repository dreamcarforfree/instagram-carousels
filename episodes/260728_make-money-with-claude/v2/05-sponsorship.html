Sponsorships

The Friction
Generic "diary-style" personal accounts are low-status and impossible for brands to sponsor. They lack clear commercial intent.
The Reframe
Design a specific, thematic channel (like personal finance) that fintech or B2B SaaS brands must sponsor to reach their audience.
Sponsor-relevance beats follower scale every single time.
