Reheating Leftovers

The Flaw
You can have Claude write flawless code or an e-book in ten minutes. But if nobody wants to buy it, you have just built a highly organized file.
The Unlock
The real unlock is identifying a buyer with an active, pre-existing transactional demand, ready to deploy capital the moment you present the solution. Using Claude merely to draft basic emails or summarize articles is like hiring a Michelin-starred chef to reheat leftovers.
Identify the flow of capital first.
