The Toddler Reframe

A generic 'day in the life with my toddler' account is just a diary. It lacks commercial intent and has minimal appeal to advertisers.
Reframe the exact same content as: "A mom who saves money raising two kids and turns it into investments." Fintech brands will pay to sponsor that concept instantly.
Sponsor-relevance beats follower scale every single time.
