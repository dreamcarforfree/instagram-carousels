PRINCIPLE 06

DATSUZOKU

Breaking formulas is also a design strategy
Datsuzoku (脱俗) breaks the expected pattern
after the system is already mastered.

Not random chaos.
Not rebellion for attention.

A single calculated break
can create freshness, memory, and status.

Break the pattern
without breaking the system.
Break the pattern without breaking the system.
