PRINCIPLE 03

SHIBUI

Simple, but never shallow
Shibui (渋い) is quiet depth.

At first glance, the design looks restrained.

But the longer you look,
the more you notice the precision:

margin,
type contrast,
spacing,
texture,
proportion,

Premium does not need to announce itself.

It becomes obvious.
Quiet depth becomes obvious.
