FUKINSEI

You may break the formula that symmetry equals stability
Fukinsei (不均整) uses imbalance to create focus.
Perfect symmetry can feel safe,
but it often feels static.

A controlled imbalance makes the eye move
without destroying order.

Tension is not a mistake.
It is direction.
Tension is direction.
