PRINCIPLE 05

YŪGEN

Showing less becomes more powerful
Yūgen (幽玄) evokes emotion through negative space without revealing everything. Ancient philosophy defines it as the mysterious beauty of the universe. In your visuals, show only 80% of the information and let the viewer's imagination fill in the rest.

Strategic withholding of information creates an irresistible pull.

Low-status design over-explains.
High-status design leaves room for discovery.
