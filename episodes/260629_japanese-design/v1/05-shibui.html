Shibui

The structure of a design that is simple but never grows tiring
Shibui (渋い) is the principle of finding the balance point between simplicity and complexity. It refers to a design that looks ordinary at first glance, but reveals precise details the longer you look at it. It serves as your standard for understated elegance when designing a timeless brand identity.
Understated elegance compounds trust over loud highlights.
