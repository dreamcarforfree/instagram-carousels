Datsuzoku

Breaking formulas is also a design strategy
Datsuzoku (だつぞく) is the principle of escaping conventional frameworks to explore new possibilities. This is not about chaotic or random design, but a conscious, calculated departure from existing formulas after you have already mastered them.
When everyone else is loud, dry simplicity is your strategy.
