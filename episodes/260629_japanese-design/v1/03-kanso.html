Kanso

Every element without a reason is noise
Kanso (簡素) is the principle of removing unnecessary elements to leave only the pure intent of your message. It is a design that leaves only function, not decoration. When a client or viewer feels "something is missing," it is actually a sign that Kanso is working. Treat that feeling as your moment for persuasive logic.
True authority is defined by what you have the courage to omit.
