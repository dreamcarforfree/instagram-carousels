Principle 02
Source: ISSEY MIYAKE

Fukinsei

You may break the formula that symmetry equals stability
Fukinsei (不均整) is the principle of creating a focal point through intentional asymmetry. The incomplete Enso (Zen circle) is a representative example, where an unfinished circle actually creates strong visual tension. This is the underlying concept when you intentionally break alignment in a layout to capture attention.
Deliberate asymmetry drives active focus and tension.
