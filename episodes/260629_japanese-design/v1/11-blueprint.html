The Visual Brand Blueprint

How to apply the principles to your brand architecture
1
Audit and Clean
Strip all decorative drop-shadows, rounded borders, and unnecessary shapes. Let typography stand.
2
Create Tension
Avoid center alignment. Push your headlines to extreme margins and leave massive empty space.
3
Establish Sharp Lines
Swap decorative shapes for sharp 90-degree corners and delicate 1px technical divider lines.
4
Design Sanctuary
Enforce the 70% negative space rule on every graphic frame to create a scroll-stopping pause.
5
Limit Contamination
Keep your color palette strictly cool-toned with electric blue and cool gray, removing warm neutral contamination.
Design is the physical structure of your brand's logic.
