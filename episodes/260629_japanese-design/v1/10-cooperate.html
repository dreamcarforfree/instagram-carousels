How Principles Cooperate

High-end design is a chemical reaction of contrasting ideas
These 7 principles can be used individually, but in high-end design, two or three operate simultaneously. The combination of Kanso (Simplicity) + Yūgen (Mystery) is a "reduce and hide" structure that feels both minimal and deep. Fukinsei (Asymmetry) + Datsuzoku (Breakaway) is an "intentional departure" structure that breaks the standard grid to capture high-status authority. Tracing how these principles clash and cooperate in references is how you move from blind copying to true understanding.
