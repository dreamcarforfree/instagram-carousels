Seijaku

Designing silence even inside busy design
Seijaku (静寂) is the principle of creating organized tranquility even in a busy environment. It is not about eliminating stimuli, but about establishing a central axis amidst the noise. A single empty space that stops the eye in a complex layout is the way Seijaku operates.
Create a visual sanctuary of calm inside a chaotic feed.
