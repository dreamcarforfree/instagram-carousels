Shizen

Hiding artificiality is also a skill
Shizen (自然) is the principle of pursuing naturalness without revealing artificiality. Just as a meticulously crafted bonsai tree is perceived as a part of nature, the goal is a design where your technical effort is invisible. Focus on the raw texture of materials over excessive graphic processing.
The highest form of skill is completely invisible.
