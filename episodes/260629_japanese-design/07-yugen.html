Yūgen

The moment when showing less becomes more powerful
Yūgen (幽玄) is the principle of evoking emotion through negative space without revealing everything. In ancient philosophy, it is defined as the deep and mysterious beauty of the universe. In your visuals, show only 80% of the information and let the reader's imagination fill in the rest.
Strategic withholding of information creates an irresistible pull.
