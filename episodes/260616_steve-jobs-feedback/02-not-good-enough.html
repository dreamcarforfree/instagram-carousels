THE POLITE TRAP
Sugarcoating Feedback
Wrapping criticism in polite compliments dilutes the standard. It leaves massive room for misinterpretation and fosters mediocrity.
THE ELITE STANDARD
Direct & Clear Critique
Point out substandard work explicitly. It is not a personal attack; it is an objective assessment to protect the team’s collective goal.
Elite performers value direct truth over polite mediocrity.
