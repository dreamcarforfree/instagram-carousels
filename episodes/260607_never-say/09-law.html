The New Law

Buyer Agency Agreements

Since August 2024, the real estate landscape has permanently changed:

01

Mandatory Written Agreements

Real estate agents are legally forbidden from showing you properties (either in-person or virtually) without a signed buyer representation agreement in place first.

02

Commission Transparency

Buyer agent commission fees are no longer published in the MLS. Written agreements explicitly outline exactly who pays what up-front.

03

Low-Risk Tailoring

You do not need to commit to a lifelong exclusive contract. Agreements can be written for a single property tour, and can be cancelled with 1-day notice.
