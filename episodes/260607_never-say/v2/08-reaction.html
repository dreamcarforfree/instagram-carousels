Home Buying Mistake 06/06

“I love this place!” or “This place is a mess.”

Never Say
"Oh my gosh, this is the one! I'll pay anything!"
The Risk: Walls have ears and eyes. Smart home cameras (Ring, Nest) are everywhere. If the sellers hear you raving, they will stand firm on price. On the flip side, over-criticizing can offend sellers and make them reject your offer out of hand.
Say This Instead
"It's an interesting option. Let's discuss our notes in the car."
The Strategy: Maintain a neutral, professional poker face during property tours. Keep your excitement and critiques private until you are completely off-property.
