Home Buying Mistake 01/06

“I make [X amount] and have [X dollars] saved.”

Never Say
"I make $200k a year and have $150k in cash savings."
The Risk: Sharing your exact cash cushion casually can work against you. If the seller’s agent gets wind of your extra assets, they’ll have zero incentive to drop their list price.
Say This Instead
"Here is my mortgage pre-approval showing I am fully qualified."
The Strategy: Let your mortgage lender handle the exact cash details behind closed doors. Keep your actual cash cushion confidential to protect your negotiating power.
