MISTAKE 1/6

Your Exact Income & Cash

Your real estate agent doesn’t need to know your salary, bank balance, or credit score—that’s what your lender is for.

Never Say

I make [X amount] and have [X dollars] saved

The Risk

If an agent knows you can afford more than your stated budget, they may push you toward higher-priced homes.

Say This Instead

Here is my mortgage pre-approval showing I am fully qualified

The Strategy: Let your mortgage lender handle the exact cash details behind closed doors. Keep your actual cash cushion confidential to protect your negotiating power.
