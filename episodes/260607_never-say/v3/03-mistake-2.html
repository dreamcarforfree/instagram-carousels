MISTAKE 2/6

Your Deadline

A tight timeline can quietly weaken your offer before negotiation even starts.

Never Say

I have to move by [X date]

The Risk

If the seller knows you are rushed, they have less reason to negotiate.

Say This Instead

We are looking for the right fit and can be flexible on timing

The Strategy: Keep urgency private. Let your agent structure dates strategically inside the offer.
