MISTAKE 6/6

Strong Reactions Inside the Home

Assume cameras and microphones may be present during a showing.

Never Say

I love this place! I’ll pay anything

The Risk

If sellers hear your excitement, they may hold firm on price.

Say This Instead

Let’s talk through our thoughts after we leave

The Strategy: Keep emotions neutral inside the home. Discuss strategy off-property.
