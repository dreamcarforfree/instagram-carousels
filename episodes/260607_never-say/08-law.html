The New Rule

Buyer Agency Agreements

Since late 2024, the law permanently changed. Here is what you must know:

01

Mandatory Prior Signing

Real estate agents cannot legally show properties (either in-person or virtual) without a signed buyer representation agreement in place first.

02

Full Transparency of Fees

Commissions are no longer listed on the MLS. Written agreements specify the exact representation services and agent compensation up-front.

03

Low-Risk & Easy Exit

You do not need to sign a lifelong contract. Agreements can be tailored for single tours, and can be cancelled with a simple 1-day notice.

The new rule is designed to protect you! Expect transparency from the start.
