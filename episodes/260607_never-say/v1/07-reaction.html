Mistake 06 / 06

Raw In-House Reactions

Walls have ears and eyes. Keep a firm poker face during physical tours.

Never Say
"I love this! This is the one! (Inside the kitchen)"
The Risk
Many sellers now have smart home cameras and active audio recordings (Ring, Nest). Saying you're in love can leak your negotiating hand to the seller.
Say This Instead
"Let's write down our notes and discuss in the car."
The Strategy
Always keep a completely neutral demeanor when inside a home. Save all excitement, criticisms, and offering strategy discussions for outside or in your car.
Keep a strong poker face during property tours. Discuss details offsite.
