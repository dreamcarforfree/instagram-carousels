THE URGENT EXIT

Urgency is a massive negotiation liability. If your personal constraints leak to the seller's side, they will hold firm on pricing and refuse concessions.

Never Say:
"I'm in a rush—I have to move by [X Date] due to [personal reason]."
Say This Instead:
"We have a strategic target timeline, but our priority is securing the right asset under the right terms."
