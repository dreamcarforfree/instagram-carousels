AUGUST 2024 NAR SETTLEMENT

The New Rules
of Representation

The law permanently changed. Here is what every buyer must know before touring a single property.

📋
Mandatory Prior Signing
Agents cannot legally show properties—physically or virtually—without a signed buyer representation agreement in place first.
💰
Full Transparency of Fees
Commissions are no longer listed on the MLS. Written agreements specify exact representation services and agent compensation up-front.
🛡️
Low-Risk & Fully Flexible
Agreements can be tailored for single tours and cancelled with a simple 1-day notice — retaining your absolute flexibility.
This is not a barrier — it's designed to protect you as a buyer.
