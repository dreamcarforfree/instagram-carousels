03 • The Flattery Bias (2/2)

Controlling the Warmth

Turn the warmth up:

AI has zero judgment. If you are stressed, you can demand absolute psychological safety.

"I am an emotionally fragile person right now. I need huge encouragement while you still help me make the right decision."

Turn the warmth off:

If you don't need your software to kiss your ass, demand truth over politeness.

"You are my ruthless mentor. Give me brutally honest feedback with zero sugarcoating."
