Fine-Tuning

Straight out of the box, a model writes work that is competent but generic. It doesn't know your service tiers or how your team writes.

Fine-tuning is taking an existing model and training it further on your own material (approved documents and correspondence) so its output matches how you actually communicate.

The clearest comparison is onboarding. A capable new hire still needs weeks of exposure before they sound like your company.
