03 • The Flattery Bias (1/2)

RLHF
(Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

Have you noticed that AI never makes you feel stupid, no matter what you ask it? That's RLHF working.

During training, human reviewers preferred being agreed with, so the AI learned to flatter your ideas by default.

But because RLHF is just behavioral training, you can easily override its personality depending on what you need.
