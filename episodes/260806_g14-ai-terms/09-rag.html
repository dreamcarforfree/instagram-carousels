07 • The Lookup

RAG
(Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

RAG is the method that connects a model to your knowledge base at the moment you ask a question.

RETRIEVAL

It searches your files for the relevant passages,

AUGMENTED

Those passages are added to the model's instructions,

GENERATION

It writes the answer from the source instead of guessing,

If a policy changes, you update the text document and the next answer is instantly correct, at zero retraining cost.

Fine-tuning is for teaching a model how to sound; RAG is for telling it what is true.
