08 • The Universal Adapter

MCP
(Model Context Protocol)

MCP is an open, universal standard that lets AI connect to any external software (calendar, email, CRM, or files).

BEFORE

Every model needed custom integration for every tool.

AFTER

One standard connector—the USB-C of artificial intelligence.

MCP support is the difference between an AI assistant that can only talk about your work and one that can actually execute tasks within your existing tools.
