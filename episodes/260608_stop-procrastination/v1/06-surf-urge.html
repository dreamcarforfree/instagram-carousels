RULE 3/4

The 10-Minute Surf

Neuroscientist Dr. Andrew Huberman explains that dopamine cravings spike right before we cave. Recognizing this wave is key to staying in control.

Instant Indulgence

Caving to distractions as soon as you feel a brief wave of boredom.

The Risk

Caving to immediate cravings trains your brain that escaping is the only solution. The second you actually indulge, your dopamine drops, leaving you frustrated.

The 10-Minute Rule

"I will wait exactly 10 minutes before giving in to any distraction."

The Strategy: This is "urge surfing." The urge is strongest right before it breaks. If you can ride out the wave for just 10 minutes, the craving naturally subsides, and you regain focus.
