THE MYTH

The "Laziness" Lie

Procrastination is not a moral failing or a lack of willpower. It is an emotional self-defense mechanism.

The Trap

"I am just lazy, unmotivated, and have no willpower."

The Consequence

Beating yourself up creates a spiral of guilt and anxiety. This increases the discomfort of the task, making you avoid it even more.

The Reality

"This task is triggering discomfort, and my brain is fleeing."

The Insight: When a task feels vague, difficult, or boring, your brain experiences a spike of emotional discomfort. Your phone, tidy desk, or closet is just the closest escape route.
