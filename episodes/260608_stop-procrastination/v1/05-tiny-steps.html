RULE 2/4

Aggressive Mini-Steps

The bigger and more daunting the goal, the more emotional discomfort it creates. Shrink the target until it is impossible to fail.

Overwhelming Target

"I need to write 10 pages today and finish the entire presentation."

The Risk

Huge milestones spike your brain's anxiety. Faced with massive friction, your brain flees to simple, high-dopamine tasks like cleaning your room or scrolling social media.

Ridiculously Small Step

"I will open my writing program and write just one sentence."

The Strategy: Commit only to the "starter phase." Open the document, or drive to the gym. Once you take the absolute smallest step, momentum starts, flow state kicks in, and the task naturally gets easier.
