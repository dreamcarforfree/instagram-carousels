THE PASSIVE CHATBOT

Wait in a browser tab until you type a prompt, then reset between sessions. The system does nothing unless you initiate the next step.

THE PROACTIVE AGENT

Lives in your machine, runs 24/7 in the background, preserves memory locally, and executes multi-step workflows autonomously.
