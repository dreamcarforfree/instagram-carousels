Level 1 — Everyday Life

The Capture Layer

When my chiropractor’s front desk printed out my schedule for the next ten sessions on a sheet of paper, I wanted a simple way to process it.

Instead of manually typing each appointment, I took a quick photo on my phone and sent a brief voice message: "Add these to my calendar."

In three seconds, my local AI agent’s vision module parsed the dates, formatted the calendar parameters, and committed all ten sessions. Zero keyboard work.

Transforming raw physical inputs into structured digital assets.
