Why #2 · Mineral Depletion

The underrated
sodium protocol

1
Electrochemical Conductivity
Your brain runs on electrical signals, and sodium is the conductor. Low sodium slows down neuron transmission. You feel this as brain fog and low motivation.
2
Replenish Before Caffeine
You lose minerals over 8 hours of sleep. A glass of water with a pinch of quality sea salt replenishes your neurons' ability to fire, often clearing fog before coffee.
It's basic neurochemistry. Morning fog is often mineral loss, not coffee deficiency.
