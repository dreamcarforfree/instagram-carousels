The Baseball Lesson
Coaches moved strategy to mornings and drills to afternoons. Players absorbed tactics faster and made sharper in-game decisions. The schedule was the only variable.

The High-Activation Risk
When repetitive drills ran in the morning, highly active brains got bored and improvised, causing form breakdowns and a spike in injuries.
3
Complexity Over Admin
The morning brain craves novelty and pattern-seeking. Repetitive, low-demand work doesn't conserve energy — it creates errors.
The morning brain is your most expensive resource. Allocate it to strategy.
