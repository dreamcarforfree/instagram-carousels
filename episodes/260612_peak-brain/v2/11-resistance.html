Implementation · Step 3

Expect the initial
friction

1
"It feels irresponsible"
When you ignore emails, Slack, or urgent requests for the first two hours of your day to protect strategic thinking.
2
"It feels unnecessary"
When you skip your morning coffee for the first 30 minutes, or drink water with a pinch of sea salt first.
3
"It feels unproductive"
When you step outside under the sky for 5 minutes after waking up, instead of opening your phone immediately.
Resistances are not failure. They are signals that the old habit loop is losing its grip.
