Implementation · Step 1

Identify your
mismatches

1
List morning tasks
Write down everything you currently do during the first 2 hours of your day. Most people discover they are on autopilot.
2
Categorize load
Label each as High-Cognitive (strategy, deep planning, hard choices) or Routine (inbox clearing, Slack, admin work).
3
Relocate the admin
Shift all routine tasks to the afternoon. Leave your morning window pristine for strategy. The fix takes less than one week.
Every routine task in your morning window is a direct tax on your peak cognitive output.
