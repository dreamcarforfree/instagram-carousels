Implementation · Step 2

The 30-day
reset protocol

1
Calibrate First Light
Step outside within 60 minutes of waking for 5–20 mins of natural sunlight. Drink salt water before coffee.
2
Decouple caffeine
Eat breakfast first, wait 30 minutes, then drink coffee. Avoid overlapping glucose and cortisol spikes.
3
Protect morning focus
Use the first 2–3 hours after waking for your single most cognitively demanding task (writing, strategy, choices).
4
Sleep-supporting carbs
Eat complex carbs (rice, sweet potato, oats) at dinner to support melatonin conversion and sleep depth.
You're recalibrating a biological system, not a productivity trick. Commit to 30 days.
