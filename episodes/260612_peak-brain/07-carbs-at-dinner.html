Why #7 · Sleep Science

Carbs at dinner
actually help you

1
The Serotonin Pathway
Complex carbohydrates facilitate tryptophan transport across the blood-brain barrier. Tryptophan converts to serotonin, the direct precursor to melatonin (your sleep hormone).
2
Closing the Output Loop
The brain needs specific chemical conditions to consolidate learning and decisions. Deep sleep tonight directly dictates your cognitive performance tomorrow morning.
This is not about eating more — it's about timing your nutrition to support sleep depth.
