The Problem

Why is your
reach stuck?

1
You broadcast to "everyone"
The feed has no room to read. Speak to one person scrolling — not a stage.
2
You sell before you serve
Trust is the asset. Selling in every post is the most expensive marketing there is.
3
Your hook assumes context
Strangers decide in 3–5 seconds. Your entry point is just too narrow.
The algorithm isn't punishing you — it's a gatekeeper for attention.
