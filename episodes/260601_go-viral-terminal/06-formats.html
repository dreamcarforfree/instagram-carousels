Why #3 · A Repeatable Format

Don't chase trends.
Master a format.

1
"Is It Worth It?"
Deconstruct a luxury item, rate the price. Expertise as trust engine.
2
"Two Characters, One Lightbulb"
Play expert + novice. Debunk a myth in a quick conversational skit.
3
"Man on the Street"
Unscripted reactions from strangers. Real, repeatable, endlessly varied.
4
"Visual Metaphors"
A wastepaper basket = emotional overwhelm. Complex made instant.
A trend gives a moment. A format gives a system.
