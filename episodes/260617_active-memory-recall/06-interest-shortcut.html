THE INTEREST SHORTCUT

Stop memorizing.
Start translating.

Translate abstract concepts into frameworks you actually care about. If you are trying to master a dry accounting system:

• Translate the balance sheet into your own monthly budget,
• Map it to your training and workout routine,
• Apply it to a business or project you actually run,
Once your brain sees real-life utility, the mechanics lock.
