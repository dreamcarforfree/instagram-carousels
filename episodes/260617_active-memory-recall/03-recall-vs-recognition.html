Your brain does not store memories like files. It must actively reconstruct neural pathways each time. Think of a trail through a dense forest:

✖
Passive Re-Reading
Like a tour guide pulling you through the trail.
✓
Active Retrieval
Walking the trail entirely alone from memory.
Every time you pull information out without looking, the pathway gets worn deeper and recall becomes automatic.
