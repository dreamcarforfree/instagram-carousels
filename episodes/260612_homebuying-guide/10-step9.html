9. Don't Skip the Home Inspection

🔍
Hire a pro inspector
Never inspect by yourself. Uncover hidden foundation, roof, plumbing, or electrical damage.
🛠️
Understand the fixes
Ask how urgent problems are, and how costly or time-consuming repairs will be.
🙋
Ask the key question
Ask your inspector: "Would YOU buy this property based on its condition?"
Protect your family and your wallet with professional safety checks.
