2. Check Your Financial Readiness

📊
Know your credit score
Request your credit report and resolve any errors before applying for a mortgage.
📝
Get pre-approved
Search lenders, compare rates, and submit W-2s/statements to show sellers you are serious.
💸
Factor in all costs
Prepare for property taxes, homeowners insurance, monthly maintenance, and potential HOA fees.
Homeownership is about much more than just the purchase price.
