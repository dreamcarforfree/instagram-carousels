3. How Much Do You Need to Save?

Housing DTI
< 28%
of gross monthly income
Housing payments should never exceed 28% of gross income.
Overall DTI
< 36%
total monthly debt
Total payments (housing + auto + cards) should stay under 36%.
Savings Target
3-20%
down payment + closing
Includes 3%–20% down, title/escrow fees, and representation fees.
Reserves
1-3%
of home price annually
Reserves for unexpected repairs and ongoing home maintenance.
Example: For a $7,000/mo income, keep total debt under $2,520 and housing under $1,960.
