4. Research Locations Carefully

🚗
Check daily commutes
Assess commute times, public transportation, and proximity to shopping or medical facilities.
🏫
School districts matter
Always check school ratings—even without kids, it heavily impacts your home's resale value.
🌳
Environmental fit
Visit neighborhoods at different times of day to check noise, traffic, and environmental humidity.
A beautiful home in a bad location can turn into a regretful purchase.
