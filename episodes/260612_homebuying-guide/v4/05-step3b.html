3. Additional Financial Considerations

Most buyers spend a significant portion of their savings on:

💵
Down payment (3%–20%)
📈
Loan points (used to buy down interest rates)
📝
Closing costs & acquisition fees
💼
Buyer’s representative fee (~3%)
🛡️
Lender-required reserves (1%–3% of purchase price annually)

Since these costs add up quickly, many buyers negotiate for the seller to pay their agent’s commission, wrapping it into financing. However, reserves are always the buyer’s responsibility.
