3. Safe Income-to-Debt Ratio

Housing DTI
< 28% of gross monthly income
Your monthly housing payments (mortgage, tax, insurance) should never exceed 28% of gross income.
Overall DTI
< 36% total monthly debt payments
Your total monthly debt obligations (housing + auto + cards) should stay safely under 36% of gross income.
Example: For a $7,000/mo income, keep total debt under $2,520 and housing under $1,960.
