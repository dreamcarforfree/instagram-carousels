7. Spend More Time at Open Houses

⏱️
Do not rush
Go to open houses every week to educate yourself about the real estate market.
🦟
Inspect neighbors & yards
Look for unkept neighboring yards, mosquito issues, lack of screens, or loud noises.
📣
Overhear local details
Neighbors often reveal hidden barking dogs, nearby construction, or concerning property backstories.
A quick 10-minute walk-through won't show you the neighborhood's true details.
